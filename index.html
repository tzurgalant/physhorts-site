physhorts

physhorts is a personal tool that generates short physics-simulation videos (ball battles and similar animations) with its own code, and uploads them to its owner's YouTube channel once a day.

It uses the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, authorized by the channel owner through Google OAuth, to upload and schedule those videos, update their titles and descriptions, and read view statistics for the same channel. It does not access any other channel or any viewer's data, and it is not offered to other users.
